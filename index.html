02 · Query

Describe the question.
The agent runs the jobs.

You don't need to know tool names. Name the places, the window and what you want to compare. Behind the scenes the agent follows the same sequence every time, which is what makes results replicable.

1

Check coverage. data_coverage confirms which days exist before a window is chosen.

2

Find places. locations_search looks up venues in the Overture Maps catalogue. aois_create turns a place into an area of interest (a point plus radius, or a polygon).

3

Run jobs. jobs_create or jobs_create_batch starts one or many independent analyses.

4

Poll and read. jobs_get_many until every job is succeeded, failed or cancelled. Results come back as structured data.

Prompts to try

"Compare Ayala Malls TriNoma and SM City North EDSA for
 25–27 Sep 2026 (Manila time). Show relative panel size,
 median dwell and how much their audiences overlap."
"For both malls, show the hour-by-hour rhythm over the same
 window and how far visitors live from each."

04 · FAQ

Common questions.

Short answers. Your MessyNet contact can go deeper on any of them.

Is this a headcount of how many people visited?

No. The data is a mobility panel sample, not a census. Treat counts as relative: one site against another, one window against another, a share of a whole. Dwell time (median and mean) is measured on the sample and can be quoted as such.

What data and dates are covered?

The panel currently spans from October 2024 to early October 2026. Coverage varies by country and by day, so the agent checks data_coverage before choosing a window. The places catalogue covers Japan, the Philippines and Singapore today, and the Philippines is where we've found density strongest.

Why did my query come back thin or empty?

Usually the window is too short or the site too small for the panel. Widen the window, use a larger radius, or compare against a busier peer. Some outputs, such as visit-type mix and socio-economic scores, need more visitors than a three-day window gives.

How long does a job take?

Most finish in seconds once a worker picks them up. Large windows can queue for longer, so keep windows tight and let the agent batch independent jobs together. Anything unwanted can be cancelled.

Do I need to know the job types or write code?

No. Ask in plain language and name the places and dates. The agent chooses from the job catalogue and never invents job types. Developers can also call the same tools from their own MCP clients.

Where do the place boundaries come from?

Place geometries come from the Overture Maps Foundation catalogue. You can also define your own area as a point and radius or a polygon, which is useful for car parks, precincts or entrances.

Does it expose personal data?

No device IDs are exposed. Where individual-level structure is needed, such as trip chains, it is keyed to anonymous person IDs from the panel. Socio-economic scores are comparable only within one country.

Can I feed results into my own workflows?

Yes. Results are structured JSON, so an agent can pass them to a spreadsheet, a report, a dashboard or another tool in the same conversation. Typical uses are site selection, tenant-mix reviews, campaign planning and before-and-after comparisons.

Who do I contact for access or a pilot?

Write to brett@messy.network with two or three sites and a time window, and we'll run a live comparison for you.
